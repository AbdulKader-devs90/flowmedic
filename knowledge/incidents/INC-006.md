# INC-006 Audit tablespace full

## Symptoms

server.log shows ORA-01653 unable to extend table on the audit tables; services slow as audit queues fill.

## Root cause

Full-pipeline audit logging on busy services grew the audit tables; no purge job and autoextend was off.

## Resolution steps

1. [read] DBA checks used and free space and autoextend on the audit tablespace.
2. [change] DBA adds a data file.
3. [change] Purge audit data older than the retention period.
4. [change] Turn off full-pipeline logging on busy services and add a free-space alert.

## Verification

No new ORA-01653; audit queue drains.
