# INC-004 IS internal DB pools time out on login

## Symptoms

Audit queue warnings, services slow, many threads in WmConnectionPool.getConnection and Oracle network reads.

## Root cause

ISInternal, ISCoreAudit and DocumentHistory share one pool; dead sessions from a forced restart plus a slow listener exhausted it.

## Resolution steps

1. [read] grep server.log for 'Login has timed out', 'fail-fast', ISS.0128; note first time and count.
2. [read] Test the internal pools in the Admin UI.
3. [read] DBA: sessions of the IS schema by machine, listener log errors.
4. [change] DBA clears dead sessions; restart the internal pools.
5. [change] Longer term: one pool per function, connection validation, dead-connection detection.

## Verification

Pool tests pass; no login timeouts for 30 minutes.
