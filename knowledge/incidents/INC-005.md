# INC-005 Leftover audit session holds a row lock across restarts

## Symptoms

After two forced IS restarts, services still hang on audit writes. The DB shows one session blocking others for over two hours.

## Root cause

A session from the killed IS process kept an open transaction on the audit table; new sessions queued behind its lock.

## Resolution steps

1. [read] DBA lists blocking sessions with machine, program and duration.
2. [read] Match the blocker to the stopped node; confirm no live IS thread owns it.
3. [change] DBA kills the blocking session.
4. [change] Add a rule: after any forced IS restart, the DBA checks and clears leftover sessions.

## Verification

No blocking sessions; audit queue drains.
